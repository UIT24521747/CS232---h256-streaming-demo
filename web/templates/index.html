SUMMARY
H256 · block-matching motion vectors, DC intra prediction, uniform quantization, zero run-length entropy coding · round-robin load balancer over two real Flask streaming nodes, scoped per video_id · independent per-user sessions (SessionManager/UserSession) · bilinear-upscale + hand-written sharpen super-resolution. No GAN, no pretrained model, no real H.265. Xem README.md để biết chi tiết từng bước.
